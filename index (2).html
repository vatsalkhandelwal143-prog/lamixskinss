❓ Questions You Asked Us
Where are we located?
Shree Amrit Plaza, Jagat Farm Market, Ground Floor, Shop No. 142, Greater Noida.
Open 11:30 AM – 9:30 PM
What devices do we wrap?
Mobile, laptop, charger, AirPods, console, controller, tablet, camera and many more. Some models may not be available in software, so please confirm before visiting.
Handcut or machine cut?
We use both methods, depending on the device and design condition.
Do we have 3D skins?
Yes, for both mobile and laptop — themes like Anime, Cars, Marvel, DC, Gods, Abstract, Nature, Travel and many more designs available.
Can you make a customized design?
Yes, we also do custom design printing — if you have your own design or photo in mind, we can print it for you.
What about quality, heating and residue?
We don't use material that leaves residue after removing the lamination, and paint won't come off with the lamination or skin. There's no heating issue. We manufacture our own skins, so we use the best quality material.
Is applying included, or is there an extra charge?
No extra charge — our prices already include the applying/service cost.
Do we have laptop screen guards?
Yes — glossy and matte finish, for 14"–17.6" and MacBook M1–M5 / Pro models and many more sizes. See the full Screen Guard price list above.
